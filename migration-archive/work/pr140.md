## Summary

Closes #133 — mid-piece key changes (途中転調) are now detected, stored on the canonical payload, and notated in MusicXML.

- **KeyChange domain type** mirrors MeterChange: a key-signature boundary on the canonical beat axis. ScoreRevisionPayload.key_changes validates that the first change sits at beat 0 carrying the head key_signature and that starts are strictly increasing; serialization is omitted when empty so legacy revisions keep their derived IDs.
- **estimate_key_segments** in transcription/key.py: per-measure duration-weighted pitch-class histograms + a DP that minimizes -sum(duration * correlation) with a per-split penalty (5% of total weight). Segments snap to measure starts, minimum 2 measures, so a one-bar tonicization never becomes a key change. Falls back to the single-key estimate for short/very long pieces.
- **Pipeline**: measure boundaries are derived from the meter map + pickup on the canonical *beat* axis (correct for 6/8 etc.), and key_changes flows into build_score.
- **MusicXML**: to_music21 emits a key signature at each change's measure (mid-measure offsets supported); the Horn in F export transposes every signature by +1 fifth.
- **setMeter edit**: key changes rescale with the beat axis alongside the tempo map, so modulations stay glued to their measures.

Verified end-to-end: a synthetic C-major -> Db-major lift at measure 17 produces keyChanges [0:C, 64:Db] through the full transcription job, and exports two key signatures (concert 0,-5 / horn 1,-4).
