## Summary

Fixes #156. A score marked Swing (issue #134 detection) auditioned and
followed with straight eighths: Verovio 6.3's renderToTimemap ignores
the <sound><swing> playback hint (verified — identical tstamps with and
without the element).

## How it works

- score/swingWarp.ts builds a written-ms -> sounding-ms warp from the
  canonical payload: swingFeel (detected offbeat phase, e.g. 2/3), the
  tempo map, and the head time signature. Written phase 1/2 maps to the
  detected phase p; the two halves scale linearly so beat onsets,
  measure boundaries and total duration are fixed points — only
  offbeat content moves. Malformed/missing fields fail open (straight
  playback).
- playbackTable.ts takes an optional warp applied to every tstamp, so
  segments, onset lookups and duration all live in sounding time — the
  cursor clock and synth need no changes.
- ScoreReadyWorkspace passes the warp on both table builds (initial
  render and re-render after edits).

Tempo conversion uses the #157 primary-beat semantics (6/8 -> dotted
quarter), matching what the corrected export writes into sound tempo.
