## Summary

* New 採譜オプション「音源の種類」(texture): `mono` / `melody` / `auto`.
* `melody`: overlap resolution keeps the top voice — a lower accompaniment hypothesis is dropped instead of clipping the melody tail — and the Basic Pitch detection band widens 880 Hz to 1400 Hz so JPOP vocal lines above the horn range are captured (the horn-fit projection still happens downstream).
* `auto` (default): cleans monophonically first; when overlap evidence (4+ different-pitch overlaps and 5%+ of events) says the source is a mix, it re-cleans keeping the top voice. Mono sources are untouched — identical results to before.
* Harmonic-ghost suppression still runs before the prefer rule, so overtone artifacts cannot cut a sustained note's tail.
* The choice is echoed in `meta.settings.texture` for reproducibility.

Refs #85 (melody extraction narrows the polyphony gap; full chord support remains open).
