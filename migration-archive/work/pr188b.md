## Summary

Completes #188: auto-estimated tempo is now checked for the classic half/double octave error, and the review surface offers a one-click fix.

## Engine

- New tempo_octave.detect_tempo_octave: censuses the ratio between median inter-onset and inter-beat intervals. IOI:IBI ≈ 1/2 suggests doubling the printed BPM; ≈ 2 suggests halving. Guards: needs >= 12 onsets / 8 beats, the winning ratio must own >= 55% of votes, and a quarter-ratio (sixteenth-writing) population above 20% suppresses the half call.
- Emits ReviewReason.TEMPO_UNCERTAIN (caution, whole-piece) only for auto-estimated tempo — a pinned BPM is the user's word. Evidence carries estimatedBpm, suggestedBpm, direction.

## Desktop

- tempo_uncertain wired into the reason union, copy-key list, and both copy decks.
- The review bar's action button applies suggestedBpm through the existing undoable setTempo edit — one click, same undo stack as every other rhythm edit.
