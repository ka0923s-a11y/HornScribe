## Summary

* §8 minimap: a 14px whole-clip strip docks at the waveform's bottom edge for clips >= 5 min or whenever the view is zoomed — short full-view clips stay clean.
* The zoom window paints as an accent band; the playhead is mirrored so position is readable at a glance.
* Click/drag centres the zoom window; when zoomed out it seeks instead. ArrowLeft/Right (5% step), PageUp/Down (20%), Home/End work on the focusable strip (role=slider with aria-valuetext timecode).
* Peaks downsample to 160 max-per-bucket columns — flat repaint cost on long files.
* The `--minimap` modifier lifts peaks/bands/handles/actions above the strip so nothing overlaps.
* New pure helper `centerViewAt` in import/selection.ts (unit-tested).
